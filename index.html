Set the number of doors, press the button, and watch the script play thousands of rounds on its own — picking a door at random, opening every losing door but one, then switching half the time and staying put the other half. The scoreboard settles the argument.

Each round: the script picks a car door at random, then a contestant door at random. It opens every remaining door that hides a goat except one — leaving exactly two closed doors. A coin flip then decides, for that round only, whether the contestant switches to the other closed door or stays. Theoretical odds for 3 doors: staying wins 1/3 of the time, switching wins 2/3 of the time.
